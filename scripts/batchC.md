## v065 Decoupling: what it is, what it costs (market insight, CTA PLAN) - Fri 14 Nov 2pm
1. You and your spouse own a condo together. Buying a second home means 20% extra stamp duty.
2. Some couples look at decoupling: one spouse sells their share to the other.
3. The one who's out may then buy as a first time buyer, without that 20%.
4. But it isn't free. Buyer's stamp duty is due on the share transferred, plus legal fees.
5. CPF used for that share goes back to CPF, and the remaining owner may need a new loan.
6. Sometimes the costs outweigh the savings. Get legal advice before you start.
7. Comment PLAN and we'll send you the calculator to run your numbers.
Disclaimer: Private property only; HDB has its own rules. Not legal or tax advice.

## v066 Selling within 4 years? Seller's stamp duty (skill, CTA save) - Fri 14 Nov 6pm
1. You bought a condo in 2025 and want to sell after two and a half years. You could owe 8% in stamp duty.
2. For private homes bought from 4 July 2025, seller's stamp duty lasts four years.
3. Sell in the first year: 16%. Second year: 12%.
4. Third year: 8%. Fourth year: 4%. After that, nothing.
5. On a 1.6 million dollar sale in year three, that's 128,000 dollars.
6. HDB flats aren't affected, because of the minimum occupation period.
7. Save this before you plan your exit.
Disclaimer: Charged on the higher of price or market value. MOF, IRAS.
