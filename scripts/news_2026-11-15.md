## v115 · Rates just ticked up. Again.
Pillar: finance (home loans). Length 35 to 40 s. Suggested style: 3D.
Source: US October CPI (Yahoo Finance/AFP, UBS, 10 to 11 Nov); overnight SORA about 1.74% on 6 Nov, highest since July 2025 (Bloomberg, Tiger Brokers); 3M SORA about 1.23% late Sep, 1.27% on 30 Oct, 1.34% on 6 Nov (latest point is one source, so the script says "above 1.3 percent"). 0.5% on 1 million dollars over 20 to 30 years is about 230 to 240 dollars a month. Re-check SORA before filming.

- 0 to 2 s (number hook): "Highest since July 2025. That is where Singapore's overnight interbank rate just went."
- 2 to 8 s (POV): "You are on a floating home loan, and you have not looked at it in a year."
- 8 to 16 s (problem): "You will only notice when the new instalment arrives."
- 16 to 26 s (insight): "US inflation came in hot, a December Fed hike looks likely, and 3 month SORA has gone from about 1.23 percent to above 1.3 percent in six weeks."
- 26 to 34 s (solution): "Know three numbers: your lock in end date, your spread, and what a 0.5 percent rise costs you. On a 1 million dollar loan, that is about 230 to 240 dollars a month."
- 34 to 38 s (CTA): "Comment PLAN and we'll send you the calculator used in this video."

Visual notes: a dial labelled SORA creeping up; a monthly bill on the kitchen table grows slightly each time the dial clicks.

## v116 · 30% more to build. Cheaper to buy?
Pillar: market (new launches). Length 35 to 40 s. Suggested style: illustrated story.
Source: JLL Global Construction Costs Index 2026, reported by EdgeProp (11 Nov) and Property and Build Asia (12 Nov): tender prices up 30.2% since 2021 vs CPI 17.7%; costs up 2.5% to 3.5% in 2026 and 2.3% to 3.3% in 2027.

- 0 to 2 s (number hook): "Up 30 percent since 2021. That is how much more it costs to build here."
- 2 to 8 s (POV): "You are waiting for the 2027 launch wave to bring prices down."
- 8 to 16 s (problem): "You assume more supply means a lower price per square foot."
- 16 to 26 s (insight): "Building costs rose 30 percent while everyday prices rose about 18 percent, and they are forecast to rise again next year. Land plus building cost sets the floor for a developer's price."
- 26 to 34 s (solution): "So instead of waiting for a big drop, shortlist the launches that fit your budget and check your exit before you buy."
- 34 to 38 s (CTA): "Most people decide this wrongly. Comment WHY to see the mistake."

Visual notes: a condo rises floor by floor, a cost tag stacking on each floor; a buyer waiting on a bench beside a calendar flipping to 2027.
