## v126 Your loan is tested at 4% (board)
- Drop In: You earn 10,000 dollars a month, and today's rate is about 1.5%. So a 1 million dollar loan feels easy.
- Direction: Here's how the bank actually decides what you can borrow.
- Solve: Your total debt payments can't pass 55% of your income: 5,500 dollars. But the bank tests your loan at 4%, not today's rate. At 4%, that 1 million dollar loan costs about 4,770 a month on paper, not 3,450. Add a car loan, and you may not pass.
- Next step: Get an in principle approval before you view, so you know your real ceiling.
- CTA: Not sure if you're on track? Comment CHECK.
- Disclaimer: Illustration, 30 year loan. MAS TDSR rules; the bank's own assessment applies.

## v134 Up to 120,000 dollars in grants (illustrated)
- Drop In: You're a first timer couple, and you think you earn too much for any housing grant.
- Direction: Here's how the biggest grant works, and how to check yours in 5 minutes.
- Solve: The Enhanced CPF Housing Grant gives first timer families up to 120,000 dollars, for new or resale flats. It shrinks as your household income rises. At 8,501 to 9,000 dollars a month, you still get 5,000.
- Next step: Apply for your HFE letter. It shows your exact grant before you choose a flat.
- CTA: Send this to the person you're buying with.
- Disclaimer: HDB grant rules. Other grants and conditions apply.

## v128 Buy first, sell later: 6 months (board)
- Drop In: You buy a 1.5 million dollar condo before you sell your HDB flat. The stamp duty bill includes 300,000 dollars extra.
- Direction: Here's how couples get that back, and the deadline you can't miss.
- Solve: As a married couple with at least one citizen, you pay 20% extra upfront on your second home. You get it back if you sell your first home within 6 months: from purchase for a completed home, or from completion for a new launch. And there are no extensions.
- Next step: Plan your sale date before you sign, not after.
- CTA: Comment PLAN and we'll send you the calculator used in this video.
- Disclaimer: IRAS ABSD remission for married couples. Conditions apply.

## v130 Your CPF comes back with interest (board, mid line)
- Drop In: You used 200,000 dollars of CPF for your flat. When you sell 10 years later, you have to put back about 256,000.
- Direction: Here's why, and how to see your real cash before you sell.
- Solve: Every CPF dollar you used goes back to your CPF account, plus the 2.5% interest it would have earned.
- Mid: Useful so far? Follow for more.
- Solve: That refund comes out of your sale price first. Your cash is only what's left.
- Next step: Check your accrued interest on the CPF website before you price your flat.
- CTA: Save this before you sell.
- Disclaimer: Illustration, compounded yearly. CPF rules.

## v124 910 dollars psf for the land alone (map)
- Drop In: 1.82 billion dollars, for one plot of land in the west, before a single brick.
- Direction: Here's what that bid tells you about the next launch prices.
- Solve: The top bid was about 910 dollars per square foot of floor area, 39 percent above the only other bid. Land plus building cost sets the floor for a developer's price. Old resale prices nearby can't tell you that.

## v129 Sell within 4 years? Up to 16% (board)
- Drop In: You bought a condo in 2025. If you sell it next year, you could lose 12% of the price in tax.
- Direction: Here's the timeline that decides how much you pay.
- Solve: Homes bought from July 2025 carry seller's stamp duty for 4 years. Sell in year 1, 16%. Year 2, 12%. Year 3, 8%. Year 4, 4%. On a 1.3 million dollar sale in year 2, that's 156,000 dollars.
- Next step: Mark your 4 year date the day you buy, and plan your exit around it.
- CTA: Save this before your next viewing.
- Disclaimer: MOF and IRAS SSD rates for purchases from 4 July 2025. HDB flats follow MOP instead.

## v127 30% of your income, not 55% (board)

- Next step: Get your HFE letter and bank approval before you shortlist, so you only view what fits.
- CTA: Not sure if you're on track? Comment CHECK.
- Disclaimer: MAS MSR rule. Loan amount is an illustration at 3%.

## v132 Your lease and your CPF (illustrated)
- Drop In: You're 35, and the flat you love has 55 years left. You plan to pay with CPF.
- Direction: Here's the one check that decides how much CPF you can use.
- Solve: Your CPF use depends on whether the lease covers the youngest buyer to age 95. At 35, 55 years only takes you to 90. So the amount of CPF you can use is reduced.
- Next step: Before you offer, ask your agent or HDB how much CPF you can use for that exact flat.
- CTA: Useful info? Follow for more.
- Disclaimer: CPF rules for the remaining lease. Conditions apply.

## v133 HDB loan or bank loan? (board)
- Drop In: You're buying a flat, and the HDB loan at 2.6% feels safe.
- Direction: Here's the one thing to know before you choose, because you can't undo it.
- Solve: The HDB loan rate is pegged to CPF, and both loans can cover up to 75%. Bank loans are cheaper today, but they move with the market. And you can switch from HDB to a bank later, but never from a bank back to HDB.
- Next step: Compare both on your own numbers, and decide how much rate risk you can live with.
- CTA: Not sure if you're on track? Comment CHECK.
- Disclaimer: HDB loan rules. Bank rates change often; refresh in the week of filming.

## v135 Joint tenancy or tenancy in common? (illustrated, mid line)
- Drop In: You're buying a home with your partner, and the lawyer asks: joint tenancy, or tenancy in common?
- Direction: Here's what each one means, in 30 seconds.
- Solve: Joint tenancy means you own the whole home together. If one passes on, the other gets it all.
- Mid: Useful so far? Follow for more.
- Solve: Tenancy in common means you each own a share, like 50 50 or 70 30. Your share goes by your will, not to your partner automatically.
- Next step: Decide this before signing. It affects your family, and later, your options.
- CTA: Send this to the person you're buying with.
- Disclaimer: General information, not legal advice.
