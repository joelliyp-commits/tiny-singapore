# News pack scripts, 4 Nov 2026 (awaiting Joel's approval)

## v104 61 years left. Still a record.
Style: board. An HDB resale board for Tampines executive flats; a lease bar under each listing drains from 68 to 61 years while the price tag climbs past 1.24 million to stop at 1.275 million dollars. Target 35s.
- Hook: "1.275 million dollars. With 61 years left."
- POV: "You own an older flat, and you think the lease is quietly eating your value."
- Problem: "Most owners assume a shorter lease always means a lower price."
- Insight: "This month, a Tampines executive flat with about 61 years of lease left sold for 1.275 million dollars, a new Tampines record. The last record, in June, had about 68 years left. Buyers paid for space, a high floor and a mature town."
- Solution: "Lease still shapes how much CPF and loan your next buyer can use. So before you sell, check who your next buyer is and how they will finance it."
- CTA: "Comment your flat type and town. We'll tell you which path fits you."
- Sources: Stacked Homes, 3 Nov 2026 (99.co headline matches the price; HDB resale data not separately checked).

## v105 Prime is only 19% more
Style: 3D. Two condo towers side by side, CCR and RCR; the CCR tower shrinks year by year from 64% taller (2007) to just 19% taller (2026). Target 35s.
- Hook: "Only 19% more."
- POV: "You are choosing between a city fringe condo and one in prime."
- Problem: "When the gap shrinks, prime suddenly feels like a bargain."
- Insight: "Prime resale condos now cost about 19% more per square foot than similar city fringe ones, the smallest gap since 1998. Five years ago it was 36%. In 2007, 64%. But last quarter, nearly 1 in 5 prime resales made a loss."
- Solution: "Compare both paths on entry price, holding period, rental demand and your next buyer, not on the gap alone."
- CTA: "Before you commit, ask yourself this one question. Comment WHY if you want it."
- Sources: Stacked Homes analysis, 2 Nov 2026 (repeated by Faribai, 3 Nov 2026); loss figure from Cushman & Wakefield via The Business Times, 2 Nov 2026.
