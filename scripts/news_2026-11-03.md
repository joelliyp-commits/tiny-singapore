## v093 1 in 5 sold at a loss
Style: board. A resale board with 20 tiles flipping from green to red, one by one, until 4 of 20 in the CCR column turn red, while the OCR column stays almost all green. Target 35s.
- Hook: "Nearly 1 in 5."
- POV: "You bought in the city because prime always holds. Ten years later, you sell."
- Problem: "Nearly 1 in 5 owners like you sold at a loss last quarter."
- Insight: "In the CCR it was 19.5%. In the suburbs, only 1.6%. Islandwide, 4.6%, the highest since 2022. Most losses were set on the day of purchase."
- Solution: "Before you buy, check how past owners in that project did when they sold."
- CTA: "Most people decide this wrongly. Comment WHY to see the mistake."
- Sources: Cushman & Wakefield data via The Business Times, 2 Nov 2026 (seen on Yahoo News SG live and Red Hot Singapore; both trace to the same BT report).

## v094 2 years 10 months to your keys
Style: map. Singapore map with three pins lighting up (Sembawang, Bayshore, Tengah), each with a countdown clock; Sembawang's clock stops first at 2 years 10 months. Target 35s.
- Hook: "2 years and 10 months."
- POV: "You want a BTO, but you cannot wait 4 or 5 years."
- Problem: "Most people pick the estate first and only check the waiting time later."
- Insight: "This November, 1,310 flats in Sembawang come with a 2 year 10 month wait. Bayshore and Tengah are about 3 years 2 months. And the income ceiling is now 16,000 dollars."
- Solution: "Decide what matters more for you, the wait, the location, or the price, then shortlist."
- CTA: "Comment your flat type and town. We'll tell you which path fits you."
- Sources: HDB Pulse 1 Nov 2026; The Straits Times (via Yahoo) 1 Nov 2026; AsiaOne 1 Nov 2026.
