## v098 Extra stamp duty: who pays what (skill, board, CTA save) - Sat 28 Nov 2pm
1. You're buying your second home as a citizen. That's 20% extra stamp duty before anything else.
2. Citizens: 0% on the first, 20% on the second, 30% from the third.
3. Permanent residents: 5% on the first, 30% on the second, 35% from the third.
4. Foreigners: 60% on any home.
5. On a 1.5 million dollar second home, a citizen pays 300,000 dollars.
6. That's on top of the usual buyer's stamp duty.
7. Save this before you plan your next purchase.
Disclaimer: IRAS ABSD rates from 27 Apr 2023. Some nationalities follow citizen rates under trade agreements.

## v103 Commission earner? The bank counts 70% (calculation, 3D, CTA PLAN) - Sat 28 Nov 6pm
1. You earn 10,000 dollars a month, all commission. The bank counts only 7,000.
2. Banks must cut variable income by at least 30%, using your last 12 months.

6. So plan your budget on what the bank sees, not what you earn.
