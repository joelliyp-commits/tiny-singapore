# Batch F (1 to 7 Dec), YAP structure. Pending Joel's script approval
Every script follows the YAP Triangle:
- **Drop In** (0 to 2s): a specific "you" moment with the number.
- **Direction**: one spoken promise of what the viewer gets by staying.
- **Solve**: 2 or 3 steps.
- **Next step**: one concrete action.
- **CTA**: frozen set only.

## v119 The bank values it lower (board)
- Drop In: The bank just valued your 700,000 dollar condo at 680,000.
- Direction: Here's what that 20,000 dollar gap really costs you, and how to catch it before you pay a cent.
- Solve: Your loan is 75% of the valuation, so 510,000, not 525,000. That's 15,000 dollars more upfront. And the 20,000 above valuation can't come from CPF. It's cash.
- Next step: Before you pay the option fee, ask your banker for an indicative valuation.
- CTA: Save this before your next viewing.
- Disclaimer: Illustration. 75% LTV, first loan. CPF rules apply.

## v121 Selling and buying HDB: the order (illustrated)
- Drop In: You want to sell your flat and buy the next one, and you're scared of being left with no home in between.
- Direction: Here's the order that stops you getting stuck.
- Solve: As the seller, register your intent to sell, then wait 7 days before you grant an option. Your buyer needs a valid HFE letter first. They pay an option fee of up to 1,000 dollars and have 21 days to exercise. After that, completion takes about 2 months.
- Next step: Line up your next home's timeline before you grant the option.
- CTA: Send this to the person you're buying with.
- Disclaimer: HDB resale process. Timelines vary.

## v113 Fixed rates jumped. Lock in now? (board)
- Drop In: 0.77 percent. That's how much some fixed home loan rates rose since September, and you're repricing next month.
- Direction: Here's how to choose between fixed and floating in under a minute.
- Solve: Fixed is now above floating. On a 1 million dollar loan, 1.9 percent instead of 1.4 percent costs about 240 dollars more a month. Floating is cheaper today but moves with SORA. Fixed costs more but stays put.
- Next step: Check when your lock in ends and how long you'll hold the loan. That decides which one fits.
- CTA: Not sure if you're on track? Comment CHECK.
- ⚑ The 0.77 point rise is one source (Cashew via EdgeProp 10 Nov). Refresh rates in the week of filming.

## v120 Resale condo: $75,000 in two weeks (3D, mid line)
- Drop In: You've found a 1.5 million dollar resale condo. Within two weeks, 75,000 dollars of your money is committed.
- Direction: Here's the order to get it right, so you don't lose it.
- Solve: First, the option fee, usually 1%: 15,000 dollars. Then, within about two weeks, you exercise with another 4%: 60,000 dollars.
- Mid: Useful so far? Follow for more.
- Solve: Stamp duty is due within 14 days of exercising. On 1.5 million, that's 44,600 dollars.
- Next step: Get your loan approval in principle before you pay the option fee. Without it, the 15,000 is at risk.
- CTA: Save this before your next viewing.
- Disclaimer: Typical terms. Each OTP sets its own periods. IRAS BSD.

## v117 Your MOP starts later than you think (illustrated)
- Drop In: You collected your BTO keys in 2021, so you're planning to sell in 2026.
- Direction: Before you start viewing, here's the one date you must check.
- Solve: Most flats have a 5 year minimum occupation period. It counts from the day you collect your keys, not from booking. For a resale flat, it counts from completion. Plus and Prime flats need 10 years, and so do ECs on land sold from May 2026.
- Next step: Check your exact MOP date on the HDB Flat Portal before you commit to your next home.
- CTA: Useful info? Follow for more.
- Disclaimer: HDB rules. Sources for the EC rule: Stacked Homes and EdgeProp.

## v118 Decoupling: the real costs (board)
- Drop In: You own a 1.6 million dollar condo with your spouse. A second home would cost you 20% extra stamp duty.
- Direction: Some couples decouple. Here are the 3 real costs before you do.
- Solve: One, the buying spouse pays stamp duty on the half. On 800,000 dollars, that's 18,600. Two, the selling spouse's CPF goes back to their account, with interest. Three, the remaining owner takes over the whole loan on their own income.
- Next step: Get legal and tax advice first. IRAS can look closely at deals done mainly to avoid stamp duty.
- CTA: Comment PLAN and we'll send you the calculator used in this video.
- Disclaimer: Illustration. Not legal or tax advice.
