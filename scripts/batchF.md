## v117 Your MOP starts later than you think (skill, illustrated, CTA follow)
1. You got your BTO keys in 2021, so you plan to sell in 2026. Check one date first.
2. Your minimum occupation period is 5 years for most flats.
3. It counts from the day you collect your keys, not from booking or signing.
4. For a resale flat, it counts from the date the resale completes.
5. Plus and Prime flats need 10 years, and so do ECs on sites from May 2026.
6. Check your exact MOP date on the HDB Flat Portal before you plan your next home.
7. Useful info? Follow for more.
Disclaimer: HDB rules. Check your own MOP date with HDB.

## v118 Decoupling: the real costs (calculation, board, CTA PLAN)
1. You and your spouse own a 1.6 million dollar condo and want a second home. The extra stamp duty would be 20%.
2. Some couples look at decoupling: one spouse buys out the other's half.
3. The buyer pays buyer's stamp duty on that half. On 800,000 dollars, that's 18,600.
4. The seller's CPF used for that half goes back to their CPF, with interest.
5. And the remaining owner must take over the whole loan alone, on their own income.
6. Get legal and tax advice first. IRAS can look closely at deals done mainly to avoid stamp duty.
7. Comment PLAN and we'll send you the calculator used in this video.
Disclaimer: Illustration. Not legal or tax advice.

## v119 The bank values it lower (calculation, board, CTA save)
1. You agree to buy a 700,000 dollar condo. The bank values it at 680,000.
2. Your loan is based on the lower number, the valuation.
3. 75% of 680,000 is 510,000, not 525,000.
4. So you need 15,000 dollars more upfront.
5. And the 20,000 above valuation can't come from CPF. It's cash.
6. Ask for an indicative valuation before you pay the option fee.
7. Save this before your next viewing.
Disclaimer: Illustration. 75% LTV, first loan. CPF rules apply.

## v120 Resale condo: $75,000 in two weeks (skill, 3D, MID FOLLOW, CTA save)
1. You found a 1.5 million dollar resale condo. Within about two weeks, 75,000 dollars of your money is committed.
2. First, the option fee, usually 1%: 15,000 dollars.
3. Then you usually have two weeks to exercise, with another 4%: 60,000 dollars.
4. Useful so far? Follow for more.
5. Stamp duty is due within 14 days of exercising. On 1.5 million, that's 44,600 dollars.
6. Get your loan approval before you pay the option fee, or you could lose it.
7. Save this before your next viewing.
Disclaimer: Typical terms; option and exercise periods are set in each OTP. IRAS BSD.

## v121 Selling and buying HDB: the order (skill, illustrated, CTA send)
1. You want to sell your flat and buy the next one. Here's the order that stops you getting stuck.
2. Sellers register their intent to sell, then wait 7 days before granting an option.
3. Buyers need a valid HFE letter before taking the option.
4. The option fee is up to 1,000 dollars, and the buyer has 21 days to exercise.
5. Then both sides submit the resale application, and completion takes about 2 months.
6. Line up your next home's timeline before you grant the option.
7. Send this to the person you're buying with.
Disclaimer: HDB resale process. Timelines vary.
