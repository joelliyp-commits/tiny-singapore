## v080 Nov BTO: Standard, Plus or Prime? (skill, board, MID FOLLOW, CTA save) - Wed 18 Nov 2pm

2. About 7,960 flats across six towns, including Bedok, Toa Payoh and Tengah. HDB confirms each project's label at launch.
3. Standard: 5 year minimum occupation period. After that, you can rent out the whole flat.
4. Useful so far? Follow for more.

Disclaimer: HDB rules. Recovery rate is a % of resale price or valuation, whichever is higher.

## v082 No HFE letter, no flat (skill, illustrated, CTA follow) - Thu 19 Nov 2pm
1. You found the perfect resale flat. But without one letter, the seller can't even give you the option.
2. It's the HDB Flat Eligibility letter, or HFE.
3. It tells you if you can buy, which grants you get, and how much HDB will lend you.
4. You need it before you apply for a BTO, and before a seller can grant you the option to purchase.
5. It takes about a month, and longer near a BTO launch.
6. It lasts 9 months. So apply when you start viewing, not when you find the flat.
7. Useful info? Follow for more.
Disclaimer: HDB. Processing times vary.

## v083 Backing out of your BTO: what it costs (skill, illustrated, CTA save) - Fri 20 Nov 2pm
1. You booked your BTO flat. Then life changed. Backing out could cost you 25,000 dollars.
2. Cancel after booking, and you lose the option fee: 500 dollars for a 2 room Flexi, 1,000 for 3 room, 2,000 for 4 room and above.
3. Cancel after signing the Agreement for Lease, and you lose 5% of the price.
4. On a 500,000 dollar flat, that's 25,000 dollars, plus legal fees.
5. You also wait a year before you can buy another HDB flat, EC or grant funded resale.
6. And any extra ballot chances you built up go back to zero.
7. Save this before you book.
Disclaimer: HDB rules. Stamp duty refund subject to IRAS.

## v086 Stamp duty: the tiers nobody explains (calculation, board, CTA save) - Sun 22 Nov 2pm
1. You're buying a 700,000 dollar flat. Your stamp duty is 15,600 dollars. Here's why.
2. It's charged in slices, not one rate.
3. 1% on the first 180,000. 2% on the next 180,000.
4. 3% on the next 640,000. 4% on the next 500,000.
5. So a 1.5 million dollar condo costs 44,600 dollars.
6. Above 1.5 million it's 5%, and above 3 million, 6%.
7. Save this before you set your budget.
Disclaimer: IRAS buyer's stamp duty, residential. Excludes ABSD.

## v081 800,000 dollars: HDB, EC or resale condo? (market insight, 3D, CTA CHECK) - Sun 22 Nov 6pm
1. You've got 800,000 dollars to spend. HDB, EC or resale condo? The rules decide more than the price.
2. Resale HDB: you can get grants, and your loan instalment is capped at 30% of income.
3. You'll live there at least 5 years before you can sell.
4. New EC: there's an income ceiling, the same 30% cap, and new sites need 10 years before you can sell.
5. Resale condo: no income ceiling, a 55% cap on all your debts, and no minimum stay.
6. But sell within 4 years and seller's stamp duty applies.
7. Not sure which fits you? Comment CHECK.
Disclaimer: HDB, MAS, IRAS rules. Eligibility varies by household.
